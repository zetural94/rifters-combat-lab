Glossary AP / BP / R1 / OA / T1
AP
action points (usually 3/turn; Dying = 2).
BP
encounter difficulty budget (sum of monsters).
R1
range 1 = adjacent (melee).
OA
opportunity attack — react to ranged-from-melee / leaving R1.
T0–T3
Power Roll tier (2d10+mod): higher total = stronger effect.
ADV/DIS
advantage / disadvantage on the Power Roll.
Help
ally rerolls the lower d10 after the roll.
Move
first Move costs 1 AP and unlocks Speed for the turn; later Moves spend remaining spaces for free.
Careful Step
always 1 AP → up to 3 spaces, no OA.
